Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · Applies to the Kinetik Android app and kinetik-dating.vercel.app

Kinetik is a dating app operated by Niladri Chatterjee, Kolkata, West Bengal, India ("Kinetik", "we"). This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use Kinetik, why we collect it, where it goes, how long we keep it and the choices you have. We have tried to write it in plain language. If anything is unclear, write to kinetik.dating@gmail.com.

1. Who can use Kinetik

Kinetik is only for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we delete any such account as soon as we become aware of it.

3. Selfie verification happens on your phone

Kinetik asks for a selfie to check that your profile photos show you. That comparison runs entirely on your device. The selfie is never uploaded and no face template leaves your phone. The only thing sent to our servers is the result: verified or not.

4. Data collected automatically

  • Account activity such as likes, passes, matches, unmatches, reports and blocks, so the app can work and so we can act on abuse.
  • Device push token if you allow notifications, so we can tell you about new matches and messages.
  • Technical logs such as IP address, request times and error details, kept for security and debugging and rotated automatically.
  • Subscription status for Kinetik Premium, described in section 6.

Kinetik contains no advertising and no advertising or analytics trackers.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to take profile photos, the verification selfie and photos in chat.
  • Microphone: to record voice notes in chat.
  • Location: to find people near you.
  • Notifications: to alert you to matches and messages.

Each permission is requested only when the related feature is first used, and the app keeps working, with that feature disabled, if you decline.

6. Payments

Kinetik Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and is the merchant of record; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. Google tells us whether your subscription is active, when it renews or ends, and gives us an order reference so we can unlock Premium and handle cancellations and refunds. Google's handling of your payment data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is shared only with the service providers that run Kinetik, each bound to process it on our instructions:

  • Other members see your profile: first name, age, photos, bio, prompt answers and approximate distance. Your email, exact coordinates and date of birth are never shown.
  • Hosting: our servers run on Oracle Cloud, with the database and cache hosted by managed providers. Some of these servers are located outside India.
  • Email delivery: Brevo sends the sign-up codes and account emails.
  • Geocoding: Geoapify turns coordinates into a place name. Only coordinates are sent, never your identity.
  • Payments: Google Play, as described above.
  • Legal requests: we disclose data when required by law or to protect the safety of our members.

8. How long we keep data

  • Your profile, photos, matches and messages are kept while your account exists.
  • Sign-up details entered before the email code is verified are discarded after a few minutes if never verified.
  • Technical logs are rotated automatically within days.
  • Blocks are kept while both accounts exist. A chat closed by a block stays stored, unseen by either member, until one of the two accounts is deleted.
  • Reports of abuse are kept as safety records. A chat that is the subject of a report is kept, unseen by either member, even after an unmatch or an account deletion, and is deleted 180 days after the report is resolved, as Indian law requires such records to be preserved. See our Child Safety Standards.
  • Payment records required for tax and accounting are kept for the period Indian law requires, even after account deletion.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account inside the app. Deletion removes your profile, photos, matches, messages and location data from our systems (except a chat that is the subject of an abuse report, which is kept as described in section 8), and cancels any active Premium subscription with Google Play so you are not billed again. If you cannot access the app, email kinetik.dating@gmail.com from your registered address and we will delete the account for you.

10. Your rights

You can access and correct your profile data inside the app at any time. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and other applicable laws you may also ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw consent, by emailing kinetik.dating@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

11. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with TLS. Passwords are hashed, chat messages are encrypted at rest, and access to production systems is restricted to the operator. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us at once if you believe your account has been misused.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a material way we will show a notice in the app before the change takes effect and update the date at the top of this page.
