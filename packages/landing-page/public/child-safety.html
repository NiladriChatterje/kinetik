Child Safety Standards

Effective 18 October 2026 · Applies to the Kinetik Android app (com.kinetik.app) and kinetik-dating.vercel.app

Kinetik is a dating app for adults, operated by Niladri Chatterjee, Kolkata, West Bengal, India. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out what is prohibited, how anyone can report it, and what we do when we learn of it.

1. Kinetik is for adults only

  • You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. Every member gives their date of birth at sign-up, and both the app and our servers refuse a date of birth that makes someone under 18. An account cannot finish sign-up without one.
  • A profile is only shown to other members when its date of birth makes the member 18 or over.
  • If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we ban it.

2. What is prohibited

The following are banned on Kinetik, in profiles, photos, chat messages, voice notes and files:

  • Any content that sexually exploits or abuses children, including child sexual abuse material in any form, real or generated.
  • Grooming: contacting, befriending or manipulating a minor with the aim of sexual contact or abuse.
  • Sexualising minors in words or images, or soliciting sexual content from or about anyone under 18.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or arranging to meet a minor for any sexual purpose.
  • Sharing links to, or asking for, any of the above.

Breaking these rules leads to immediate removal of the account and a report to the authorities.

3. How to report

In the app: tap the flag button on a member's profile card in Discover, or in the top bar of your chat with them. Choose Report, pick Child sexual abuse or exploitation or May be under 18, add any details, and send. You can block the member at the same time. The person you report is not told who reported them.

  • By email: write to kinetik.dating@gmail.com with the member's name and what you saw.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call the police on 112 or Childline on 1098, and report online at cybercrime.gov.in.

4. What we do when something is reported

  • Every report is stored and sent to our safety inbox, where a person reviews it. Reports about a child's safety are marked urgent and reviewed first.
  • When we find child sexual abuse material or exploitation, we ban the accounts involved as soon as we become aware of it. A banned account is signed out within minutes, cannot log in or sign up again with the same email, disappears from every part of the app, and its chats end.
  • A chat that is the subject of a report is kept, even if either member unmatches or deletes their account, so that it can be reviewed and given to investigators. Once the chat has ended, neither member can open it again. It is deleted 180 days after the report is resolved, in line with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.
  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and to the police, as the POCSO Act requires, and we cooperate with law enforcement requests.
  • Blocking takes effect at once: the two members no longer see each other anywhere in the app, and their chat closes.

5. The law we follow

We comply with the child safety laws that apply to us, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.
